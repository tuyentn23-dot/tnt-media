## Target
- Format: YouTube Shorts

- Tone: curiosity + light comedy
- Setting: everyday Vietnamese street-side drink shop
- Core goal: maximize retention until reveal/payoff

## Scene Plan

### Scene 1 - Hook
0-2s: crowded Vietnamese street-side drink shop, one empty red plastic chair conspicuously unused in the center, people nearby avoid it. Quick push-in toward chair.

### Scene 2 - First attempt
2-7s: young adult approaches and begins to sit. A nearby glass of iced tea trembles slightly. Person freezes and steps back.

### Scene 3 - Second attempt
7-14s: another customer approaches. An orange street cat suddenly runs from under a nearby table. Customer jumps back.

### Scene 4 - Skeptic arrives
14-22s: confident man pulls the chair into clear view while surrounding customers turn to watch.

### Scene 5 - Build suspense
22-27s: close-up as the man starts sitting. Keep cracked leg hidden from viewer.

### Scene 6 - Reveal
27-34s: shop owner rushes in and stops him. Reveal that one chair leg is badly cracked and about to break. Payoff: there is nothing supernatural; the chair is simply unsafe.

### Scene 7 - Comedy ending
34-40s: man looks embarrassed; orange cat calmly jumps onto the chair. Quick freeze-frame ending.

## Visual Consistency Rules
- Same red plastic chair in every scene.
- Same Vietnamese drink-shop environment.
- Same primary human characters across matching scenes.
- Same orange cat across scenes 3 and 7.
- Natural Vietnamese clothing and environment.
- Realistic cinematic look.
- No logos or copyrighted characters.
