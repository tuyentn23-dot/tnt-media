# Vilevi Controlled Rebrand Experiment v1

## Objective
Test whether the existing @vilevi5676 channel can be repositioned toward AI-enhanced visual entertainment/storytelling while preserving the value of its ~7,000 existing subscribers.

## Evidence Baseline
- User-reported subscribers: ~7,000
- Recent public sample: 15 videos
- Average views: 3,379.4
- Median views: 2,261
- Maximum views: 19,282
- Minimum views: 77
- Initial decision: KEEP_AND_TEST

## Rules
1. Do not delete or hide old videos during this experiment.
2. Do not change the channel into a narrowly defined niche before results exist.
3. Publish only a small coherent batch first.
4. Each video must be original and traceable by content ID.
5. Avoid copyrighted characters, celebrity imitation, living-artist imitation, or reused unlicensed media.
6. Human approval is required before publishing.
7. Measure every experiment using the same core metrics.

## Test Batch
Create 6 short-form videos grouped into 3 concepts, 2 videos per concept.

### Concept A — Curiosity Visual Story
Short, surprising visual stories based on familiar everyday situations with one unexpected twist.
Goal: preserve compatibility with Vilevi's historically broad curiosity/lifestyle content.

### Concept B — Vietnam Reimagined
Original AI-enhanced cinematic/fantasy interpretations of Vietnamese places, food, memories, or everyday culture.
Goal: use recognizable local context while introducing a distinctive visual identity.

### Concept C — Recurring Original Character
Introduce one simple original recurring character in humorous or emotional 30–60 second micro-stories.
Goal: test the foundation for a reusable long-term IP rather than one-off viral clips.

## Publishing Sequence
1. A1
2. B1
3. C1
4. A2
5. B2
6. C2

Do not post the six videos all at once. Keep enough separation to evaluate response from each upload.

## Metrics
For every test video record:
- impressions when available
- views
- views after 24h
- views after 72h
- average percentage viewed / retention when available
- likes
- comments
- shares when available
- subscribers gained/lost
- traffic source when available
- Shorts feed distribution when available

## Decision Rules
### Continue direction
A concept is promising if it repeatedly performs above the recent channel median and shows acceptable retention/engagement relative to the other test concepts.

### Expand
If one concept clearly wins across both videos, produce a second batch of 6–10 videos around that concept before making a major channel rebrand.

### Reject
If a concept performs materially worse than the recent baseline twice, stop that concept rather than forcing it.

### Hard rebrand
Only consider full name/banner/about-page repositioning after a new concept demonstrates repeatable performance.

## Next Production Step
Develop the six concrete concepts, hooks, scripts, asset plans, titles, and production requirements. No publishing action is authorized yet.
